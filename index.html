About Me

Where commerce meets creativity

A first-year Commerce student with a media specialist’s eye — building a bridge between business studies and the world of digital content.

Port Said, Egypt YW

“Commerce by study — storytelling by passion.”

Port Said University Faculty of Commerce — English Section

I’m Yaqeen Wael, a first-year student at the Faculty of Commerce, English Section, Port Said University. While my academic path is rooted in business, my creative energy lives in digital media — from cutting and producing videos, to animating ideas with motion graphics, to designing visuals that speak before words do.
